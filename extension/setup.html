Sarjy

The browser companion to assist your usage

1 Allow the microphone

Allow microphone access to enable voice commands.

2 Check the agent service

Verify that the agent service is accessible and properly configured with the Access Key.

✓ Use it

Pin the extension (puzzle icon in the toolbar), then click Sarjy's toolbar button to start talking. Clippy appears in the top-left corner of the page; click him to show or hide the conversation. Click the toolbar button again to end the call.
